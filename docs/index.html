    How much of a country ranking is real?

    Puts the published standard errors back in: 95% intervals, rank ranges, pairwise significance tests and inverse-variance weighting for ten headline indicators. Most pairs of systems differ, but almost no neighbours in a ranking do, and the near-census systems (Luxembourg, Macao, Malta) can dominate any precision-weighted average.
