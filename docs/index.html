    Do richer schools really have better tech? It depends what you ask.

    Seven school-resourcing measures, split by rich/poor, city/rural and private/public schools. Raw device counts often favour poorer or rural schools, but reported shortages, poor quality and readiness to teach digitally clearly favour richer and private ones. Plain-language grid plus a per-country lookup.

    How are schools governing devices and AI, and is it changing?

    A nine-item policy-intensity index, plus the four device-policy regimes compared 2022 vs 2025 with each system's shift tested against its own standard error and classified as a fast adopter or lagging. Includes open questions on whether phone bans track with less in-school leisure device use or less AI use.
