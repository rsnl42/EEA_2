How do students split their device time between learning and leisure?

PISA 2025, up to 91 education systems. Students reported hours per day on digital devices for learning and for leisure at school, before/after school on weekdays, and on a weekend day. The OECD text gives the OECD averages; this compares learning with leisure country by country, splits the weekday between school and home, and looks at the heavy-use tails. 90 of 91 systems are covered: United States* has no time-use values in the OECD table, so it is absent everywhere on this page.

OECD-average hours per day

Weekday total = at school + before/after school. A weekend figure is for one weekend day, so no weekly total is implied.

Four time-use archetypes

Ward clustering on weekday device time, weekend device time and the learning share of device time in each of the three contexts (silhouette 0.24, bootstrap stability 0.63). Clustering the six raw hour figures was unstable (stability 0.33–0.45) because they mostly measure overall device intensity.
