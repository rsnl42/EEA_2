PISA 2025, up to 91 education systems. Table 4.47's nine 2025 policy items rolled into a policy-intensity index; table 4.49's four device-policy regimes compared 2022 vs 2025, with each system's shift tested against its own standard error.

Regulatory depth, 2022 vs 2025

Depth = 0×(neither) + 1×(bans-only or guidelines-only) + 2×(both), averaged across schools — 0 means no school reports either policy, 2 means every school reports both. Each dot-pair is one system; drag your eye from the left dot (2022) to the right (2025). Colour marks whether that system's shift on the "both" regime is significant at |z|>1.96, using the S.E. the workbook already reports for the change — see the caveat below about a few systems with mechanically tiny S.E.s.

Policy-intensity index

Average z-score across all nine table 4.47 items (written statement, phone ban, subject guidelines, teacher-set rules, co-set rules, responsible-use programme, social-network policy, teacher-collaboration programme, scheduled collaboration time). Higher = a more formalised policy environment across the board, not just bans.
