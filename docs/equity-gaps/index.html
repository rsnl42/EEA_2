Gaps between student and school groups across up to 90 education systems in PISA 2025. Select any cell to see the gap country by country.

OECD-average gaps

💡 How to read this heatmap (Hover over any column header or cell for details)
1. Big Number (+5.2 / −3.1)
The OECD-average gap between the two groups.
• Positive (+): 1st group (e.g. Boys) is higher.
• Negative (−): 2nd group (e.g. Girls) is higher.
2. Small Numbers (34 · 2)
Number of countries with a significant gap.
• Left (34): Countries where 1st group is higher.
• Right (2): Countries where 2nd group is higher.
3. Cell Shading & "Not Using" Columns
• 🟦 Blue shading: 1st group higher in most countries.
• 🟥 Red shading: 1st group lower in most countries.
• Note: For "Not using" columns, a positive gap (+) means the 1st group uses AI/devices less.
