Does school guidance keep pace with student AI use?

PISA 2025, up to 85 education systems. How many students use AI for schoolwork, against how many are asked in lessons to judge what AI tells them. OECD averages first.

Source: OECD, PISA 2025 Results (Volume I), chapter 4 tables I.B1.4.53, 55, 67 and 68. "Used AI" means at least one of four schoolwork purposes; "AI guidance" means students said they assess the quality of chatbot-generated information in lessons at least sometimes. Use and guidance are country shares from different questions, not the same students, and the comparisons are correlational across systems. Groups compare each system with the OECD average; "significantly" means a 95% confidence interval that excludes it. Systems marked * carry comparability flags in the source.
